# 90-day launch plan: nvox + the lab

One story, told everywhere: **a small lab that prints in-house, says yes or no fast,
and logs every step.** Every channel below tells that same story in its own format.
Nothing here posts on its own. You post it.

Before day 1, fill the `{{placeholders}}` in `voice.md`: your name, region, number of
clinics, turnaround in days. Several drafts can't go out until those are filled in.

## How the channels connect

```
           shoot once (delivery day, a print, a case sent back)
                                  |
       YouTube long video (weekly, 6-10 min)    <- the anchor
          |              |               |
   blog post         3 Shorts         LinkedIn posts
   (/yt-seo text,    = 3 Reels        (/li-repurpose)
   expanded)         (/yt-shorts)          |
          |              |               |
          +-------> email list <---------+
                         |
                 clinic DMs / calls  ->  cases in nvox
```

One recording a week feeds YouTube, the blog, Reels, Shorts and LinkedIn. You never
start from a blank page on four platforms.

## Who each channel is for

| channel | who it reaches | what it's for | cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | clinic owners, practice managers, other labs | trust, inbound cases, nvox pilots | 3-4 posts/week |
| Instagram | dentists, dental techs, assistants | proof you can see: prints, fits, delivery | 3 Reels + 1 carousel/week, daily stories |

| Dentaltown, FB dental groups, r/DentalLab | dentists and techs in discussion | answers, not ads | 3 helpful replies/week |

## Phase 1: set up (days 1-14)

**Profiles.** Make every profile say the same thing in one line, for example
"Small dental lab. Night guards, flippers, models. Cases logged end to end with nvox."
- `/li-profile`: paste your LinkedIn profile, get a score and rewrite.
- `/ig-profile`: same for Instagram. Pin the three posts that prove the most.
- `/yt-audit`: once you have three videos up. Until then: channel banner, one-line
  description, a link to the blog.

**Blog: where to put it.** Put it on your own domain (`yourdomain.com/blog`), not on
Medium or LinkedIn articles, so the search traffic builds up on a site you own.
- Simplest: **Ghost** (blog + email newsletter built in, about $9-15/month), or
  **WordPress** if you already have a site there.
- If you have no domain yet, register one first. Ask me to check name availability.
- Pages you need on day 1: Home (what the lab does + nvox in two lines), Services
  (night guards, flippers, models, ortho coming), "Send a case" (how to start),
  Blog, Contact.

**Google Business Profile.** Free, and probably the fastest way to get local cases.
Category "Dental laboratory", service area, hours, 10 photos of devices and models
(no patient info), and a link to "Send a case".

**Email list.** One signup box on the blog: "Get the case checklist clinics use so
the lab accepts it first time." The checklist is the lead magnet. Build it as the
Week 1 Friday carousel and also export it as a PDF.

**Recording kit.** Phone, cheap tripod, clip-on mic, a clean bench with good light.
That's all you need.

### First six anchor videos (each becomes a blog post)

1. **"10 night guards, zero complaints: how we print them"**: KeySplint Soft, model
   first, fit check, handoff. (Proof already exists.)
2. **"Why your lab case got sent back"**: the missing info that stalls cases. Pairs
   with the checklist lead magnet.
3. **"Lab cases by text are a liability"**: show a case going clinic, to lab review,
   to accepted, with the nvox log. This is the nvox demo without calling it a demo.
4. **"Making a flipper, start to finish"**: delivery-day footage.
5. **"In-house printing vs sending out: what it does to turnaround"**: real numbers
   only, once you have `{{turnaround}}`.
6. **"Setting up the LuxCreo printer"**: only once it's real.

Blog titles should match what people type into Google: "how to send a case to a
dental lab", "night guard turnaround time", "3D printed night guard vs milled",
"what a dental lab needs with an impression". `/yt-seo` writes toward these.

## Phase 3: turn attention into cases (days 46-90)

- **Warm DMs only.** Anyone who comments twice or saves a carousel gets a
  message: `/li-dm` or `/ig-dm`. Never pitch someone cold in a comment.
- **"First case" offer.** Clinics that haven't sent you work get one night guard at
  cost, with the model, through nvox. You get proof, they get to try it at low risk.
- **nvox pilot.** 3 clinics run every case through nvox for 30 days. Ask each for one
  sentence you can quote, with their written OK (voice.md rule).
- **Ask for reviews.** Every happy clinic gets a link to your Google Business Profile.
- **Collab.** One joint video or post with a dental-tech creator or educator from your
  engagement list (`/ig-dm` collab pitch).
- **Day 30, 60 and 90 audits:** paste your analytics into `/li-audit`, `/ig-audit`,
  `/yt-audit`, and `/yt-retention` for the YouTube retention CSV. Do more of what
  worked and drop what didn't.

## What to measure

Followers come last. In order:
1. **New clinics sending cases** (the only number that pays).
2. Inbound DMs, emails and "send a case" form fills.
3. Email signups.
4. Saves and shares (IG), comments from clinic owners (LinkedIn), watch time (YouTube).
5. Followers.

Write the number for each in a short note every Sunday.

## Rules that apply everywhere

From `voice.md`: no patient anything, no clinic names without written OK, no
"HIPAA compliant" claim, no distributor-as-partner claim, never post who has paid.
Run every draft through `/li-human` or `/ig-human` before it goes out.
