## My positions

1. Lab cases sent by text and phone calls aren't a workflow, they're a
   liability. If nobody can show who approved a case and when, it isn't
   compliant.
2. A lab saying "no, send it back" quickly beats a lab saying "yes" and
   remaking it later.
3. Small labs that print in-house and buy material direct can beat big labs on
   turnaround.

## Off limits

  patient. Show devices and models only, never with labels.
- No clinic names or logos without the clinic's written OK.
- Never post about who has or hasn't paid.
- Don't claim nvox is "HIPAA compliant" or any certification unless it has
  been formally checked. Say "every step is logged" instead.
- Don't present Keystone, LuxCreo or any distributor as a partner or sponsor
  unless there's a signed agreement. Naming the material/printer you use is fine.
